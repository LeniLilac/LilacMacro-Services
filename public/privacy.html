LLilacMacro
PRIVACY

Diagnostics are optional and temporary.

LilacMacro works locally without uploading captures or logs. A diagnostic archive is uploaded only after you explicitly choose to send it.

What is retained

The service stores archive metadata, an opaque random object key, application version, declared size and checksum, and rotating abuse-prevention pseudonyms. It does not store raw IP addresses or hardware identifiers.

Retention

Routine archives expire after 72 hours. Archives over 3 GiB require a manual upload and administrator acceptance within 30 minutes. Accepted archives can be extended for at most seven days.

Private storage

Archives remain in private object storage, are downloaded only for debugging, and are never automatically extracted or served as public content.
