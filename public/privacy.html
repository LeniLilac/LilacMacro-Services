Privacy without guesswork.

This Privacy Policy explains how LilacMacro ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the LilacMacro Windows app, website, control service, and related support tools (the "Services"). LilacMacro performs its automation locally. Optional online features are separated so you can decide what leaves your device.

Effective date: August 15, 2026. Last updated: August 15, 2026.

Your privacy choices

The app presents three separate choices before the first macro session. Online features and product telemetry are initially shown on; automatic error reports are initially off. No data covered by those choices is sent until you save the screen. You can change each choice later in Settings.

  • Online features. Controls requests for signed service status, public game codes, maintenance notices, safety disables, official update metadata, and available service-backed sharing features.
  • Product telemetry. Controls bounded events used to understand reliability, feature use, expedition reward observations, and OCR performance.
  • Automatic error reports. Controls automatic creation and upload of a limited light report or an existing Deep Debug archive after an error.

Online features

When online features are enabled, LilacMacro may request signed operational status, public game codes, known-bug or maintenance disables, official GitHub release metadata, and service-backed configuration sharing when available. Turning this choice off prevents new LilacMacro service and update-check requests. One bounded request already in flight may finish and update its local public cache. Local automation remains available, but it will not receive current maintenance or safety status.

Product telemetry

When telemetry is enabled, the app sends only fields from a fixed schema: app version; feature and outcome identifiers; expedition material and quantity observations; error-state counters without free-form exception text; OCR duration and CPU or GPU execution category; Windows version; logical processor count; and process capability.

Telemetry does not include screenshots, OCR text, file paths, Roblox credentials, private-server links, Discord webhook URLs, raw hardware identifiers, or raw IP addresses. We do not sell personal information or use telemetry for targeted advertising.

The app creates a random installation UUID locally. The service immediately converts that UUID and the normalized source network into separate, monthly rotating HMAC pseudonyms. It stores neither the raw UUID nor the raw IP address. The network pseudonym is used only for a daily abuse quota. Stored telemetry and diagnostic pseudonyms use separate HMAC domains and are not directly joinable in the database.

We use telemetry to estimate active installations, understand feature use and error-prone states, improve OCR performance, and inform manually reviewed expedition reward research. Aggregates never automatically change macro behavior, reward distributions, or remote control policy. Telemetry is deleted after no more than 90 days.

Automatic error reports

Automatic reports are a separate opt-in and are off initially. With Deep Debug off, an error may create a temporary light report containing a sanitized event timeline, non-secret display and plan counts, coarse device information, model and profile identifiers, macro actions, and at most 32 important frames that the app already captured. The archive has an 80 MiB hard limit.

Light reports exclude OCR text, free-form exception text, file paths, private-server links, Discord data, and credentials. Frames can still contain personal information visible inside Roblox, so review what may be on screen before enabling automatic reports.

With Deep Debug active, the completed Deep Debug archive is sent instead. Temporary light files are removed after the upload attempt. A successfully sent Deep Debug archive is removed; a failed Deep Debug upload remains local so you can inspect or retry it. When automatic reports are off, the app does not create a light report and does not automatically send or delete your Deep Debug archives.

Manual diagnostic uploads

Settings includes a separate manual upload control. It remains off until enabled and always requires you to choose a specific ZIP file. Routine archives expire after 72 hours. Archives over 3 GiB require a short-lived administrator grant and, if accepted, may be retained for no more than seven days. Diagnostic archives remain in private object storage and are never served as public content.

Information kept on your device

Settings, plans, visual profiles, local logs, and Deep Debug archives stay on your Windows device unless a feature described above sends them. Uninstalling LilacMacro does not silently erase user-created datasets or retained diagnostics. You can remove local data with ordinary Windows file management.

Service records and providers

The service keeps limited operational records such as object keys, checksums, app versions, audit entries, and rotating abuse-prevention pseudonyms. It does not store raw IP addresses. We may share only the information needed to operate the Services with infrastructure and authentication providers, including Hetzner, Cloudflare, PostgreSQL hosting, Backblaze B2, GitHub, and Discord. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the project, or the public.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, applicable legal obligations, security, and dispute resolution. The specific telemetry and diagnostic limits above control where they are shorter. We use measures such as HTTPS, access controls, pseudonymization, private object storage, bounded schemas, and limited credentials. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. You may also withdraw an optional app choice at any time in Settings. Because telemetry identifiers rotate and the service does not retain the raw installation UUID, we may be unable to identify a telemetry record as yours. Email us to make a privacy request; we may ask for reasonable verification before acting on it.

Children's privacy

The hosted Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information through the Services, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. Those countries may have data-protection rules different from the rules where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as the Services change. We will update the date above and, for a material change to an in-app data choice, present a new notice version before the affected feature resumes sending data.
